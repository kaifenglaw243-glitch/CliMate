🚨 HO On-call

Quick-reference framework. Always follow current hospital SOP, Malaysian CPG and senior clinician instructions.

🫀 Chest Pain

  1. ABCDE + vital signs
  2. 12-lead ECG early
  3. IV access + bloods
  4. Troponin according to local protocol
  5. Assess ACS, PE, aortic dissection and other life-threatening causes
  6. Escalate if unstable or ECG concerning

🫁 Shortness of Breath

  1. ABCDE
  2. SpO₂ + respiratory rate
  3. Oxygen if indicated
  4. ECG, CXR, FBC, U&E ± ABG/VBG
  5. Consider asthma/COPD, pneumonia, pulmonary oedema, PE, pneumothorax
  6. Reassess response

🦠 Sepsis

  1. Recognise deterioration early
  2. ABCDE + lactate where appropriate
  3. Blood cultures before antibiotics if this does not delay treatment
  4. Start appropriate antimicrobials according to local protocol
  5. Fluid resuscitation when indicated
  6. Identify and control source
  7. Escalate if shock or organ dysfunction

🩸 Major Bleeding / PPH

  1. Call for help
  2. ABCDE
  3. Two large-bore IV cannulas if possible
  4. FBC, coagulation, group & crossmatch
  5. Identify source and treat immediately
  6. Activate major haemorrhage protocol when indicated

🧠 Reduced GCS

  1. Airway + cervical spine consideration
  2. Breathing + circulation
  3. Check capillary glucose immediately
  4. GCS + pupils
  5. Look for seizure, stroke, infection, intoxication and metabolic causes
  6. Urgent escalation if deterioration

⚡ Hyperkalaemia

  1. ABCDE
  2. Urgent ECG
  3. Repeat/confirm potassium if appropriate
  4. Look for ECG changes
  5. Start emergency treatment according to severity/local protocol
  6. Treat underlying cause and consider renal replacement if refractory

💊 Drugs

High-yield quick reference. Verify exact dose with current local protocol.

Magnesium Sulfate

Use: Prevention/treatment of eclamptic seizures; fetal neuroprotection in selected preterm births.

Monitor: Respiratory rate, reflexes, urine output, clinical toxicity.

Dose must be checked against current Malaysian/local hospital protocol.

Labetalol

Use: Hypertension in pregnancy.

Important: Monitor BP and maternal heart rate; consider contraindications such as significant asthma/bradycardia.

Nifedipine

Use: Antihypertensive therapy in pregnancy; may also be used for selected obstetric indications according to protocol.

Tranexamic Acid

Use: Antifibrinolytic used in major bleeding including selected postpartum haemorrhage protocols.

Adrenaline

Use: First-line drug for anaphylaxis.

Principle: Give promptly when anaphylaxis is suspected and follow local emergency protocol.

Salbutamol

Use: Bronchodilator for acute asthma/bronchospasm.

Monitor: Tremor, tachycardia and potassium in severe treatment.

🔬 Investigations

ECG — Quick Approach

  1. Rate
  2. Rhythm
  3. Axis
  4. Intervals
  5. P waves
  6. QRS
  7. ST segment
  8. T waves
  9. Compare previous ECG

ABG / VBG

  1. pH
  2. PaCO₂
  3. HCO₃⁻
  4. Primary respiratory vs metabolic disorder
  5. Compensation
  6. Oxygenation
  7. Consider lactate / anion gap where relevant

FBC

  • Hb — anaemia/polycythaemia
  • WBC — infection/inflammation
  • Platelets — thrombocytopenia/thrombocytosis
  • Look at trends, not just a single value

U&E / Creatinine

  • Na⁺
  • K⁺
  • Urea
  • Creatinine
  • Assess renal function and electrolyte abnormalities

LFT

  • ALT / AST
  • ALP
  • Bilirubin
  • Albumin
  • Interpret pattern and clinical context
